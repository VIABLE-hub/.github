# Community conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Critique ideas and code, not individuals. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing and disclosure of confidential information are not
acceptable. Respect differing experience levels and preserve contributor attribution.

Report incidents privately to the repository maintainer. If that is unsuitable, use
https://viable-id.com/contact to request a confidential escalation without posting
sensitive details. An uninvolved organization owner should review the report, hear
relevant parties and choose a proportionate response. Maintainers may remove harmful
content or restrict participation. An organization owner not involved in the report handles escalation.
